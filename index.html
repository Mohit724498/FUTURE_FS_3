About the Clinic

Healing Built on Trust, Science & Compassion

Arogya Wellness Clinic was founded in 2008 by Dr. Rajiv Mehra with a simple belief: every patient deserves the same quality of care that top hospitals offer, at a neighbourhood clinic close to home.

Over 15 years, we have grown into a trusted family clinic serving thousands of patients across the city. Our team of doctors, nurses, and health advisors work together to provide personalised, evidence-based care.
